# URI Faculty Development Training Calendar: AI, Writing, and Promotion/Tenure

Draft date: June 2, 2026

Purpose: A curated, yearlong menu of free or membership-enabled trainings from NCFDD, EAB, CUR, CIRTL, and AAC&U for URI faculty. The emphasis is on practical AI use, sustainable scholarly writing, and promotion/tenure readiness.

Note on source documents: the three Google Docs shared as a starting point required Google sign-in from this workspace, so their contents could not be read directly. This draft is built from public provider pages and URI-access evidence that could be verified online.

## Shortlist to Advertise First

These are the highest-confidence, strongest-fit items to put in a faculty-facing announcement now.

1. NCFDD 14-Day Writing Challenge: July 6-19, August 3-16, and September 21-October 4, 2026. Free; URI NCFDD membership verified.
2. CIRTL Critical AI Literacy for Teachers and Students: July 13 and July 20, 2026. Free; practical AI literacy and disclosure.
3. CIRTL UDL + AI Design Studio: June 26-July 31, 2026. Free/no network fee for CIRTL network participants; capped, so advertise early.
4. AAC&U AI Week recordings, especially Writing and Writing Instruction in the Era of AI. Free/open per AI Week listing.
5. EAB Construction Kit for Faculty AI Resources. Strong self-paced toolkit; confirm URI partner login route.
6. ACE/EAB An Equity-Minded Approach to Faculty Tenure and Promotion. Strong P&T recording; public recording page.
7. NCFDD member-library recordings on daily writing practice and academic time management. Use for early-career faculty and P&T preparation.
8. CUR COEUR 2.0 webinar and CUR mentoring webinar archive. Good for faculty mentoring undergraduates and documenting mentoring in annual review/P&T.

## Suggested Announcement Copy

URI faculty have access to a set of national faculty-development resources through institutional memberships and public/free partner programming. This year's curated pathway emphasizes three priorities: using AI thoughtfully in teaching and scholarship, building sustainable writing habits, and preparing strong annual-review and promotion/tenure evidence. Recommended starting points include NCFDD's 14-Day Writing Challenges, CIRTL's summer AI workshops, AAC&U's AI Week recordings, EAB's faculty AI resource toolkit, and selected NCFDD/CUR/EAB recordings on writing, mentoring, and promotion/tenure.
